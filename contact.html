FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?
It depends on scope. A simple AI integration or website can take 2–4 weeks. A full end-to-end product typically runs 6–12 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline after our first call.
Do you work with early-stage startups?
Absolutely. Some of our best work has been with early-stage teams who needed to move fast and build smart. We're comfortable with ambiguity and help you figure out what to build first.
What does "low maintenance" actually mean?
It means we build solutions that don't need constant babysitting. Automated monitoring, self-healing processes, clear documentation, and handoff training — so your team can own it confidently after we're done.
Can you work with our existing tech stack?
Yes. We don't force you to rewrite everything. We assess what you have, identify what needs to change, and integrate new solutions cleanly into your existing systems.
