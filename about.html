About Us

Built by People Who Care About Your Growth

We started Flowlite because we saw too many good businesses stuck — not for lack of ambition, but for lack of the right tools and the right guidance.

Our Story

We Started Because Businesses Deserved Better

Most businesses are run by talented people doing brilliant work — but spending half their day on things a computer could do in seconds. Endless spreadsheets. Missed follow-ups. No visibility into what's actually working.

We exist to fix that. Not with complicated tech, not with jargon, but with simple tools that make your business run the way it always should have.
